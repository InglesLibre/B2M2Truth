Speaking: Crime and Punishment

Task: Work in pairs. Discuss what punishment, if any, should be given to the criminals from Slide 3.

Useful language:

  • Giving opinions: "In my opinion..." / "Personally, I think..."
  • Agreeing: "That's right." / "I agree up to a point."
  • Disagreeing: "I really don't think so." / "I completely disagree."

Example: "A shoplifter should do community service rather than go to prison."

Class 1 - Slide 7

Grammar Discovery: Passive Voice

Read these sentences from the article. What do they have in common?

  • "The evidence we obtain will not be accepted in court."
  • "Most of what we do could be done by the clients."
  • "She drops her voice so as not to be overheard."
  • "Her mobile phone rings and she is called away."

Discuss with your partner:

  • What is the structure of the passive? (be + past participle)
  • Why do we use the passive? (focus on the action, not the person)
  • Where is the agent (doer) in these sentences?

Rule: Passive = be + past participle. Use it to focus on the action, not the person who did it.

Speaking: Antisocial Behaviour

Task: Work in pairs. Discuss these questions.

  1. How serious is antisocial behaviour where you live?
  2. What types of antisocial behaviour are most common?
  3. What methods are used to deal with it? Are they effective?

Useful language:

  • "In my opinion, the most effective method is..."
  • "I strongly believe that..."
  • "That's a good point, but..."
